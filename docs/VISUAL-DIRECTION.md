# ORIGEN — Visual Direction v2

## Intro 3D

The first state is an archaeological/architectural composition, not a finished logo floating in space.

### Composition
- Full-screen 16:9 desktop composition with adaptive 9:16/mobile crop.
- Winter Sierra / alpine-mountain background with warm sunrise.
- Stone terrace and circular carved pedestal centered in lower third.
- ORIGEN emblem formed by distinct stone pieces hovering above the pedestal.
- Initial state: pieces visibly separated in a readable X/Y-like composition; gaps are intentional and large enough to communicate that the user must assemble them.
- Pieces remain suspended, not touching the pedestal.
- The final assembled emblem locks directly over the same center axis.

### Materials
- TIERRA: warm terracotta sandstone.
- TIEMPO: pale limestone.
- MANO: cool anthracite slate with recessed handprint.
- Strong physical stone relief, cracks, bevels and edge wear.
- Subtle golden rim light and floating particles; avoid neon/glow-heavy CGI.

### Interaction
1. Enter in separated-pieces state.
2. User drags pieces toward magnetic targets.
3. Piece snap is tactile and elegant.
4. On completion: short silence/pause, the complete emblem becomes the hero.
5. Camera pushes through the central opening / transitions into landing.
6. Landing continues the same materials, light and territory language.

### UI
- Minimal UI only.
- Do not place a static ORIGEN logo over the pedestal during the puzzle state.
- Labels for TIERRA / TIEMPO / MANO may appear close to pieces and fade as pieces lock.
- Audio control discreet.

## Landing

The landing is the architectural continuation of the intro, not a separate visual world.

### Hero
- Use the winter/sunrise landscape as a visual anchor.
- ORIGEN typography large, restrained, editorial.
- Copy: “Construimos sobre lo que el tiempo nos dejó.”
- CTAs minimal, premium and high contrast.
- Keep stone / cream / terracotta / slate material language.

### Sections
- Projects should feel like architectural case studies, not generic SaaS cards.
- Philosophy: TIERRA / TIEMPO / MANO.
- Materials viewer uses real stone-inspired PBR surfaces.
- The 3D symbol should be reused as a visual signature.

### Responsive
- Desktop: wide cinematic composition.
- Tablet: preserve pedestal + emblem hierarchy.
- Mobile: vertical cinematic composition; emblem remains readable and centered.

## Anti-goals
- No generic dark-tech aesthetic.
- No excessive glassmorphism.
- No giant floating logo covering the puzzle.
- No flat placeholder geometry when a real asset is available.
- No unrelated imagery between intro and landing.
